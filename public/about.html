Firm Profile

Koshti & Company is a Partnership Firm of Chartered Accountants registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) under Firm Registration Number (FRN) 142857W. Established in 2015, the firm provides structured professional services in the areas of Statutory Audit, Internal Audit, Goods & Services Tax (GST) compliance, Direct Taxation, and Corporate Secretarial filings.

Operating from New Ranip, Ahmedabad, the practice serves sole proprietorships, partnership firms, closely-held private companies, and non-resident clients across Gujarat and western India.

Regulatory & Ethical Standards

The firm adheres strictly to the Code of Ethics and professional conduct guidelines mandated by the Council of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. Information rendered across all communications is factual and informative, maintaining independence and technical precision.
